LangSwap - type Hebrew or English without switching the keyboard

INSTALL
1. Drag LangSwap.app into your Applications folder.
2. Open Terminal and paste this line, then press Enter:
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LangSwap.app
   (The app is free and not from the App Store, so macOS blocks it the first time.
    Other way: try to open it, then System Settings > Privacy & Security > "Open Anyway".)
3. Open LangSwap. You will see "אA" in the menu bar.
4. Allow it in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility (turn LangSwap ON).
   The warning sign next to "אA" goes away after a few seconds.
5. Click "אA" and turn on "Start at login".

NEEDS
- Both keyboards: Hebrew + ABC (or U.S.). System Settings > Keyboard > Input Sources.
- macOS 13 or newer. Apple Silicon and Intel.

USE
- Just type. Words typed in the wrong keyboard are rewritten in the right language,
  and the keyboard switches. Words before them stay as they are.
- Typos are fixed when you finish a word. Words with capitals (names) are never touched.
- Cmd+Z right after a fix = undo (and that word is never "fixed" again).
- Double-tap Shift = convert the last words by hand, or convert selected text.
- When you click into a text field, a small EN / עב badge shows the current keyboard.
- "אA 🔒" = paused: another app is protecting a password field.

Everything runs on your Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere.
